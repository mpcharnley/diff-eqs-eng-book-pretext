Exercise 3.

Using the mks units (meters-kilograms-seconds), suppose you have a spring with spring constant \(\unitfrac[4]{N}{m}\text{.}\) You want to use it to weigh items. Assume no friction. You place the mass on the spring and put it in motion.
🔗

(a)

You count and find that the frequency is \(0.8\) Hz (cycles per second).What is the mass? (Be careful with the units here, the frequency is given in cycles per second, not radians per second.)
🔗
Answer.
\(\frac{4}{(2\pi (0.8))^2}\) kg \(\approx .158\) kg
🔗
🔗
🔗

(b)

Find a formula for the mass \(m\) given the frequency \(\omega\) in Hz.
🔗
Answer.
\(m = \frac{4}{(2\pi \omega)^2} = \frac{1}{\omega^2 \pi^2}\)
