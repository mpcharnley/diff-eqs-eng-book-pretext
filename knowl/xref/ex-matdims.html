Exercise 8.

Assume that \(A\) is a \(3\times 4\) matrix.
🔗

(a)

What must the dimensions of \(B\) be in order for the product \(AB\) to be defined?
🔗
Answer.
\(4 \times n\) for any \(n\)
🔗
🔗
🔗

(b)

What must the dimensions of \(B\) be in order for the product \(BA\) to be defined?
🔗
Answer.
\(n \times 3\) for any \(n\)
🔗
🔗
🔗

(c)

What about if we want to compute \(ABA\) or \(BAB\text{?}\)
🔗
Answer.
Must be \(4 \times 3\)
🔗
