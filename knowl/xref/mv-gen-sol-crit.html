Exercise 1.

Consider a mass and spring system with a mass \(m=2\text{,}\) spring constant \(k=3\text{,}\) and damping constant \(\gamma=1\text{.}\)
🔗

(a)

Set up and find the general solution of the system.
🔗
Answer.
\(C_1e^{-t/4}\cos\left(\frac{\sqrt{23}}{4}t\right) + C_2e^{-t/4}\sin\left(\frac{\sqrt{23}}{4}t\right)\)
🔗
🔗
🔗

(b)

Is the system underdamped, overdamped or critically damped?
🔗
Answer.
Underdamped.
🔗
🔗
🔗

(c)

If the system is not critically damped, find a \(\gamma\) that makes the system critically damped.
🔗
Answer.
\(\gamma = 2\sqrt{6}\)
