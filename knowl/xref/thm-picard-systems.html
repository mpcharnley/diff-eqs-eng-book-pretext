Theorem 4.1.14. Picard’s theorem on existence and uniqueness for systems.

If for every \(j=1,2,\ldots,n\) and every \(k = 1,2,\ldots,n\) each \(F_j\) is continuous and the derivative \(\frac{\partial F_j}{\partial x_k}\) exists and is continuous near some \((x_1^0,x_2^0,\ldots,x_n^0,t^0)\text{,}\) then a solution to (4.1.1) subject to the initial condition \(x_1(t^0) = x_1^0\text{,}\) \(x_2(t^0) = x_2^0\text{,}\) …, \(x_n(t^0) = x_n^0\) exists (at least for some small interval of \(t\)’s) and is unique.
