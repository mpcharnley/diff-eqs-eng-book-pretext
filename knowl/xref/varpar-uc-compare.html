Exercise 25.

Consider the differential equation \(y'' - y = e^x\)
🔗

(a)

Using variation of parameters, find a particular solution.
🔗
Answer.
\(\frac{1}{2}xe^x - \frac{1}{4}e^x\)
🔗
🔗
🔗

(b)

Find a particular solution using undetermined coefficients.
🔗
Answer.
\(\frac{1}{2}xe^x\)
🔗
🔗
🔗

(c)

Are the two solutions you found the same? What is going on?
🔗
Answer.
They differ by a \(Ce^x\) term.
